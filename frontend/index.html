The Solution: Our control plane strictly executes sandboxed Chromium browser navigation (CDP), ATS extraction, and spreadsheet compilation inside dedicated hardware microVMs. Pre-execution safety hooks intercept hostile exploits before guest dispatch, and snapshot checkpoints enable instantaneous sub-80ms rollback.
